About the Founder

Chandan Prajapati is the Founder of ZENTAP, an emerging technology brand focused on NFC-enabled products and digital connectivity solutions. He is currently pursuing a BCA with a focus on FinTech and Artificial Intelligence, combining his academic foundation with a strong passion for technology, innovation, and entrepreneurship.

Chandan brings a multidisciplinary skill set spanning digital marketing, graphic design, video editing, AI-powered creative workflows, branding, and strategic media management. Alongside product engineering, his background as a digital creator (Innerxframe) allows him to blend deep technical knowledge with sharp visual communication, building solutions that solve real-world connectivity challenges.

His experience in the Army and Navy NCC programs instilled a deep commitment to discipline, operational rigor, and teamwork. At ZENTAP, Chandan's mission is to bridge the divide between physical business cards and modern digital profiles, delivering seamless, contactless networking experiences for creators and enterprises across India.
