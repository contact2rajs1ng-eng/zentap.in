How It Works

One tap. Four things happen at once.

No app, no typing, no scanning a confusing QR. Someone taps your card on their phone and your profile opens instantly.

01

They tap your card

An NFC chip inside the card, standee or tag activates the moment it touches a phone, whether Android or iPhone. No app required.

02

Your profile opens instantly

A dynamic page loads with your photo, business details, WhatsApp button, booking link and portfolio, all in your own branding.

03

They take one action

Save your contact, message you on WhatsApp, book an appointment, or leave a review, whichever you've set as the priority.

04

You see it happen

Every tap and click is logged to your dashboard: who's engaging, when, and which action they took, so you know what's working.
